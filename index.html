1,000+ Staffers From Rival AI Labs Just Asked Washington for a Brake Pedal

Employees from OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, Google, and nearly a dozen other labs co-signed a letter asking the U.S. to help build tools that could “deliberately pace” AI progress — a rare moment of agreement from inside the frontier, arriving the same week Wall Street had its own AI-spending panic attack.

Over 1,000 staffers from AI labs including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google signed a “Pacing the Frontier” letter calling on the U.S. to help build tools that could deliberately slow AI progress as the world nears autonomous AI research. The list spans nearly a dozen labs, including Anthropic co-founders Jack Clark and Chris Olah and chief scientists from OpenAI, Meta, and Thinking Machines. Rundown AI

The statement stops short of calling for a pause — instead it asks for an international effort giving governments and labs the option to slow down if needed. It pins the risk specifically on automated AI research, warning that models improving other models could accelerate progress “beyond our ability to understand or control.” Both OpenAI and Anthropic endorsed the letter on X. Rundown AI

The timing is notable: the same day, investors sent the Nasdaq 100 briefly into correction territory after a sell-off in memory chipmakers and storage providers spread from Asian markets to Micron, Sandisk, and Dell. Insider caution and market jitters are, for once, pointing the same direction. Morning Brew

Columnist | Daily Dad

This Is What You’re Going to Do With It?

You get them, increasingly, for only bits and pieces. A few minutes in the morning before school. The period after practice or the wind-down before bed. You get Saturday and Sunday mornings, sure, and those are lovely. You get a week or two here and there on vacation.

As your kids get older, you find you are getting them less and less — you are fighting with more and more things for even just a little of their time. You’re fighting against sports, against their friends, against summer jobs, against them wanting “everyone to just leave me alone!” There’s not much we can do about that, except enjoy those early years while you have them.

But you know what you can not do? You can not waste these brief moments now on stupid things that don’t matter. You only get them for so many weekends and you’re spending it at the office? You’re insisting on running errands when what you need could easily be delivered for a few dollars? You’re staying up late on your phone and then grouchy on those weekend mornings?

C’mon. You only get so much time with them. This is how you’re going to spend it? You say you miss them, that it’s going by so fast — but this is what you’re doing? What they need isn’t your anxiety about the time you’ve lost. They need your presence, your attention, your time — and not later. Now. Daily Dad

Build Interactive Lead Magnets with Canva Code

Create a fun, interactive lead magnet for your company without learning to code — useful for impressing prospects and earning more business. Here’s the step-by-step:

  1. Open Canva AI, choose </> Code, and start a new project.
  2. In ChatGPT, describe your offer, target customer, and packages, and ask it to mock up a five-question assessment that helps each visitor find the best fit.
  3. Ask ChatGPT to turn that into a Canva Code prompt with the questions, answer choices, recommendation rules, results, CTA, brand colors, and voice.
  4. Paste the prompt into Canva Code, generate the site, tweak text/colors as needed, then publish and share.

Pro tip: to collect emails, add a separate Canva Form connected to Canva Sheets — test a submission before sharing to catch any gaps.

AI • Judicial System

UAE Rolls Out What It Calls the World’s First AI Court Platform

UAE Vice President Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed announced a new AI platform to be integrated across every stage of the country’s court system.

The platform will read case files, surface relevant laws and precedents, draft court documents, and provide legal analysis — with humans reviewing everything and final rulings still left to actual judges. The first phase begins in September, with the rest phased in over 18 months. It’s an easy sell for court systems buried in paperwork, but AI seeping into the legal reasoning behind rulings is a line no other legal system has crossed yet. Rundown AI
