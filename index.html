In With the New: Fresh Tariffs Hit 99% of US Imports as Temporary Levies Expire

The US slapped new 10–12.5% tariffs on 60 trading partners — covering 99.4% of all US imports — just as a 150-day temporary global tariff expired. Invoking forced-labor law, the White House says two more waves are coming. Trading partners from the EU to Australia are calling the move unjustified.

The Trump administration slapped fresh tariffs on 60 trade partners — including China, Canada, and the EU — just as the clock ran out on a 150-day global 10% tariff Trump imposed in February after the Supreme Court blocked his original “Liberation Day” levies. The new duties range from 10% to 12.5% and invoke Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, which targets countries with “unfair trade practices” — here defined as failure to curb forced labor. Morning Brew

Oil, natural gas, and many goods not made in the US are exempt. Items like aluminum and steel, which already face Trump-era national-security import taxes, won’t be doubled up. The White House is also preparing two additional waves of Section 301 tariffs: one targeting excess manufacturing capacity, and one aimed at what Trump called the EU’s “illegal and highly unethical” fines on US tech giants.

The chorus of pushback was immediate. Australia called the new tariffs “completely unjustified.” The EU said they were “not really grounded.” New Zealand accused the US of “looking for any way to put a tariff rate back on.” Brazil vowed to appeal to the World Trade Organization. A group of small businesses sued yesterday to stop the levies. Economists warn the “triple whammy” of tariffs, Middle East energy spikes, and AI spending is feeding inflation angst.

Cyclospora Parasite Outbreak Spreads to 9 States, Traced to Shredded Lettuce

Federal health officials linked four more states — Illinois, Kansas, Oklahoma, and Pennsylvania — to the cyclospora outbreak tied to shredded lettuce, bringing the affected state count to nine. The diarrhea-causing parasite has been connected to Mexican-grown lettuce from Taylor Farms, including some supplied to Taco Bell.

The FDA said Thursday it was also investigating a separate outbreak of unknown origin that had sickened 72 people. North Carolina health authorities said parsley and cilantro may also be culprits in some cases. A weekend of wondering whether it’s safe to eat a salad, per Morning Brew.

Paramount Delays $111B Warner Bros. Discovery Takeover Until June 2027

Paramount agreed to delay its $111 billion takeover of Warner Bros. Discovery until next June while a federal judge considers an antitrust suit brought by a dozen states seeking to block the deal.

The merger would combine two of the largest legacy media companies — Paramount (CBS, MTV, Nickelodeon) and Warner Bros. Discovery (HBO, CNN, Discovery+) — into a content giant aimed at competing with Netflix and Disney. The antitrust challenge centers on concerns about reducing competition in streaming and linear TV. The delay extends uncertainty for both companies’ employees and shareholders.
