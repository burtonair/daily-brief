Make Editable Product Mockups With GPT-6.1 Terra

In this guide, you'll use the new GPT-6.1 Terra to build a product mockup in Blender — creating two color options and a wide campaign image, then saving the scene so you can handle client changes without starting over. Full guide.

  1. Save an empty Blender project and connect Terra through ChatGPT Work's Computer Use plugin. Keep its access limited to your practice project.
  2. Give Terra the guide's carton dimensions and colors. Check the size, then add the product name and label as editable text.
  3. Add a camera and studio lights, then render a small preview. Check that the lettering is readable, the edges look right, and the carton sits on the ground.

Axiom's AI Agent Swarm Formally Verifies Math's Longest-Ever Proof

AI math startup Axiom completed a fully machine-checked version of the "Classification of Finite Simple Groups," rebuilding the decades-long, 15,000-page effort in 24 days using an AI agent swarm. The Rundown AI

The theorem catalogs the "periodic table of symmetry" — a full list of the basic pieces every symmetry pattern in math breaks down into. More than 100 mathematicians spread the original proof across 500+ papers, and Axiom says no single expert has ever read it cover to cover.

Axiom broke the work into 108 milestones, then dozens of agents produced roughly 43 million lines of Lean, the language that lets a computer verify each logical step. The AI caught one false claim along the way and patched it using 1976 work by mathematician Ronald Solomon.
