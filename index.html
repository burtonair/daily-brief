Columnist | Daily Dad

They Have a Pressing Claim

There are a lot of people that want your time. People at work. People on the phone. People from down the street. They need stuff. They want you to do stuff. And you do your best to do it — to do your duty as a professional, as a colleague, as a citizen and a friend. But there is a line…there has to be a line.

Theodore Roosevelt, when he was governor of New York, was famous for reserving the evenings for his family. He was busy, sure, but as Doris Kearns Goodwin quotes in her book The Bully Pulpit, Roosevelt believed his kids had “a decided claim [on his time], even in the midst of the most pressing affairs of the State.” If our kids don’t have a pressing and decided claim, who does? If we prioritize everyone else over them, what does that say?

Especially when we so often claim that we do it all for our family. Your kids deserve your time. They are entitled to your time. You deserve time with them. You are entitled to time with them, too. But you’re going to have to fight for it and protect it. You’re going to have to insist on it, even in the midst of pressing affairs and conflicting obligations.

As it says in The Daily Dad entry for March 22: your kids are not a distraction from your work. They are your work. Daily Dad

Turn ChatGPT Into a Team of Useful Agents With Raft

Turn your Claude or ChatGPT projects into a workspace for AI agents by building a three-agent team with Raft. Here’s the step-by-step:

  1. Go to raft.build, create a new server, and install Raft on your computer — you’ll need Codex CLI or Claude Code installed on the machine you connect.
  2. Connect the onboarding agent to your Codex or Claude Code subscription and choose the runtime you want your agents to use.
  3. Complete onboarding with Cindy, Raft’s onboarding assistant, which inspects your projects, skills, and integrations to configure your workspace.
  4. Ask Cindy to create a channel and a three-agent team: a lead to coordinate, a researcher to gather inputs, and a maker to build the deliverable.

Pro tip: tell the team lead to create tasks for teammates or set up recurring tasks — you can even connect agents running on different computers to the same workspace.
