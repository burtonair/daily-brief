Private Credit Faces Highly Public Questions

JPMorgan’s Jamie Dimon has warned private credit could spark the next financial crisis, once memorably calling the sector a “cockroach” after two private-credit-backed firms collapsed — but the $1.3 trillion market has yet to be tested in a real downturn. Morning Brew

Private credit means loans from investment funds rather than banks — often riskier and higher-interest than bank loans, with funds typically holding them to maturity. The boom traces back to post-2008 banking regulations that pushed riskier borrowers to alternative lenders; major private equity firms like KKR, Apollo, and Blackstone built large lending businesses on top of it. Morning Brew

The US private credit market grew from ~$500 billion in 2020 to ~$1.3 trillion by December 2024, per Bloomberg, pulling in retail investors along the way. But roughly 20% of private credit loans went to software companies, per Axios — raising fears about AI making that debt obsolete — and firms like Blue Owl have limited investor withdrawals and sold assets to cover redemptions. Morning Brew

Why it matters: Withdrawal requests have calmed down and even Dimon has softened his tone, calling the risk non-“systemic” — but observers remain split on whether the worst has passed or is still ahead. Morning Brew

Fractional Horse Ownership Puts Your Money in the Race

Companies like Commonwealth and MyRacehorse let investors buy a share of a racehorse for as little as $50 to $100, with profit-sharing if the horse wins big.

When Authentic won the 2020 Kentucky Derby, he had ~4,000 co-owners through MyRacehorse; when Mage won in 2023, nearly 400 co-owners shared in the payout via Commonwealth. But both platforms are upfront that part of what buyers are paying for is the fun of the experience — not exactly stable income. Morning Brew

Collectibles

How to Tell If Your Trading Cards Are Trash or Treasure

The trading card market is booming, with rare Pokémon and sports cards breaking multimillion-dollar auction records — but grading determines everything.

Graders like PSA and CGC score cards 1–10: a 10 needs perfectly sharp corners, centered imagery, intact gloss, and zero stains, while even one wonky corner can knock a rare Charizard down to a six. Companies won’t grade altered cards, and buffing out blemishes doesn’t help.

Fraud is rising alongside demand: the share of card submissions found to be counterfeit spiked 250% last year, per PSA. Morning Brew

Digital Assets

Web Addresses Can Be Worth More Than IRL Ones

Domain flipping has boomed since the 1990s, and the current gold rush is in .ai addresses — up tenfold to 1 million registered domains in five years.

Cars.com sold for a record $872 million in 2014, and AI.com went for $70 million this year. Bot.ai fetched $1.2 million on domain marketplace Sedo.

The biggest winner may be Anguilla: the Caribbean nation of ~15,000 people owns the .ai domain code and made $39 million from .ai registrations in 2024 — nearly a quarter of its government revenue. Morning Brew
