Reflection and Mistral Launch Open-Weight Models to Rival China's AI Lead

Two Western AI labs — Nvidia-backed Reflection AI and France's Mistral — released open-weight models this week aimed at closing the gap with China's leading open models, a shift that could reshape competition among OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google as all three prepare for public listings. Morning Brew Rundown AI

Reflection AI, financially backed by Nvidia, launched its first model, Beam, built for coding and agentic tasks, while Mistral debuted Mistral Large 4, nicknamed "Le Chonk" for its 1 trillion parameters. Unlike closed models, open-weight versions can be downloaded and customized with proprietary data, and are cheaper to run than the difficult-to-budget closed alternatives. Morning Brew

On performance, Rundown AI's numbers put China's Kimi K3 still on top for coding at 68%, with Le Chonk at 62% clearing Beam's 44%. Mistral claims a top-5 global cybersecurity ranking and 82% on a bug test that Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra mostly refused, plus a legal benchmark score nearly 3x GPT-6 Astra on Harvey's test. Weights for both models go fully public October 27. Rundown AI

Why it matters: Western businesses and governments have been wary of China's open models over security concerns, leaving room for Reflection and Mistral to fill the gap — right as OpenAI and Anthropic, already under security scrutiny, prepare to go public. Putting powerful, tweakable models into wider circulation cuts both ways: Mistral's VP of Science argues broader availability actually helps researchers audit and improve defenses. Let OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra Compare Websites For You

Use Astra in ChatGPT Work to compare software plans for a small team — checking prices, user limits, and billing terms before getting a short, sourced recommendation. Full guide here.

  1. Open Work in the ChatGPT desktop app, choose Astra, and open the built-in browser beside your chat. Keep website approvals on.
  2. Give it three pricing pages and your needs — team size, required features, and budget. Ask for monthly billing and rule out trials or purchases.
  3. Start with one site. Check that Astra reads the right billing period and includes the full team cost before letting it compare the others.
  4. Ask for a short recommendation using the checked facts. Keep missing details visible and review the final price yourself before buying anything.

Pro tip: Set the stopping point in your first request — ask Astra to finish with a comparison and leave signups, sales forms, and purchases out of the task.
