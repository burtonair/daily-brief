You Don’t Need the Third Thing

You saw it and you took care of it. Dishes in the sink. Laundry in the pile. Your partner was tired, so you took the kids. Dinner was coming up and you made all the decisions. A birthday was coming up and you planned it. Great! You did it! But now you want an award? You want a parade?

Praise is always nice, but it’s sort of pathetic how desperately we want it…for doing our job.

In Meditations, Marcus Aurelius refers to this as wanting the “third thing.” You did something good. Your family benefited. Isn’t that enough? It should be.

You noticed what needed to be done and you did it. You made someone’s day easier. You took something off your partner’s plate. You showed up for your family. That’s the reward. You don’t also need applause for it.

Give Grok Bot Its Own Email Inbox

In this guide, you’ll give Grok Bot an email address and use it to turn project updates into a short brief — checking a changed deadline and reviewing a reply before anything is sent.

  1. Open Grok Bot and set a rule that asks before sending or forwarding email. Keep personal inbox connections out of this first test.
  2. Ask Bot to claim its new inbox and confirm the address — access is rolling out, and teams need admin enablement.
  3. Send the guide’s practice emails from an account you control, and ask Bot to show each subject and a quote to confirm it read all three.
  4. Ask for a project update and a reply draft. Check the approved launch date, completed work, and missing details before approving any send to yourself.
  5. Send the changed-deadline email and ask for an update — check that the new date replaces the old one while the other facts stay unchanged.
