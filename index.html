AI Labs Head to the White House to Talk Safety — After Their Own Agents Broke Into Companies

OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, and Google are being called in to review a new voluntary framework for testing whether frontier models can hack, days after OpenAI and Anthropic disclosed breaches carried out by their own AI agents.

The White House has invited the four labs to review a newly finished framework for voluntary cybersecurity testing of frontier models. Designed under Trump’s June 2 executive order, it would let companies voluntarily give the government access to frontier models up to 30 days before release. Rundown AI

Tuesday’s meeting is where the four labs review the finished framework and its classified benchmark, and hash out implementation — including unresolved questions like what counts as “frontier AI,” whether open models are covered, and who actually runs the testing. The push comes as the EU’s AI Act takes effect and more than 1,200 AI staffers have called for the industry to slow down. Rundown AI

The catch: participation is still optional, so the framework only works if labs actually volunteer, and with the standards classified, no one outside the room will know who showed up or what they agreed to. Rundown AI

Build a Project Room Where Humans and Agents Work Together

Set up a team of AI agents that you and your teammates can collaborate with inside a free, open-source chat app called Buzz.

  1. Go to buzz.xyz and download Buzz (Mac only), then connect your Codex or Claude Code account during setup to power the agents.
  2. Tell the onboarding agents about your role and main projects. Have them break each project into its own chat “channel.”
  3. Give the lead agent one clear project and let it delegate — e.g. “Have Research Agent find five AI productivity case studies. As it researches, draft a webpage that presents each one consistently.”
  4. Follow each agent’s activity inside the channel, and ask for screenshots or work files sent back for a quick review.

Pro tip: once your agent team is set up, invite human teammates and clients into project channels just like you would in Slack. Full guide Rundown AI
