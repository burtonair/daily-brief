Do They See You Doing This?

You come up with fun activities. You come up with adventures. You love to plan things, to show them things that will light them up. Maybe they see that you do this. Maybe they appreciate it.

But you know what matters more? Do they see you having fun? Do they see you lighting up?

You planned a day at the pool, but you never got in. You took everyone to Disney, but you were the responsible adult all week. You cart them around to every activity and sport they want to play, but have they ever seen you play?

Life is meant to be lived. It should be fun. It shouldn't be a burden. It shouldn't be boring. Prove that to your kids. Show them what it looks like — show them that adulthood isn't horrible. Experience the fun things together. Not only will you create memories with and for them, but you'll show them what it looks like to enjoy life. You'll show them that growing up doesn't mean the fun has to stop.

Turn a Messy Plan Into a Shared Board

Claude Whiteboards turns a plan you talk through or paste in into an editable board your team can work from together. Here's how to build one:

  1. In Claude's sidebar, click Artifacts, then the Whiteboard tile under "Make something new." A blank board opens.
  2. Click Chat at the top of the board and talk your plan through with the microphone, or paste it in. Include owners and any dates you know.
  3. Read Claude's reply and the board it builds. Claude fills gaps with its own guesses, so check every owner, date, and arrow against what you said.
  4. Fix small things with the toolbar at the bottom. To rework one area, drag a box around it, click Chat in the toolbar, and describe the change.
  5. Click Share, invite teammates by email, and choose Editor, Commenter, or Viewer. Invites start on Commenter.

Pro tip: Before you invite anyone, settle the dates Claude guessed with their owners — on one test board, two later tasks hung off a single guessed date.
