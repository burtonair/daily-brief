Turn Sales Call Notes Into Follow-Up Emails With AI

In this guide, you'll try Inception's new Mercury 3 to turn three sales call notes into follow-ups — checking prices and promises, changing a customer's needs, and comparing the result with your usual model. Full guide.

  1. Open OpenRouter Chat and choose Inception's Mercury 3. Check the usage cost and start a fresh conversation without connected accounts.
  2. Paste the guide's fictional product facts and three call notes. Ask for a plan recommendation and a short, unsent reply for each lead.
  3. Check every recommendation against the offer — a plan that fits the team size can still miss a required feature, and a requested discount is not an approved one.
  4. Change one lead from eight users to 18. Mercury should withdraw its earlier recommendation because the largest listed plan only covers 15 people.
  5. Try the same task with your usual model. Compare mistakes, waiting, and time spent fixing the drafts before deciding which to keep.

Pro tip: Measure the time to a reply you would actually use. Fast text is less useful when it needs a long edit.

NBA Partners With Polymarket and Kalshi to Police Prediction-Market Betting

The league is partnering with the two prediction markets to protect "the integrity of the game," amid recent sports-betting scandals. Morning Brew

The NBA will get final say on what markets are offered and will bar wagers on single plays like first basket or opening tip, plus bets on players expected to play fewer than 12 minutes — both easily manipulated. The league will also get access to Polymarket and Kalshi trading data to help flag suspicious activity.

But the move comes as the Supreme Court agrees to consider whether states can ban prediction markets altogether, with state regulators arguing they're just sports betting in federally-regulated commodities trading's clothing.
