Lead Them Toward Progress

The big breakthrough of his life, Steve Jobs famously said, was realizing that most of the rules and conventions of life were just made up by people — people who were not particularly bright and certainly quite fallible. Most of the practices we follow as parents aren't nearly as old, or as scientifically grounded, as we assume. Once we realize this, we can stop being so hard on ourselves and stop being bullied by the expectations or judgments of others.

But more importantly, we have to teach our kids what Steve Jobs' parents helped him realize: that things should be questioned, that we need not be deferential to authority, and that we shouldn't take limitations or impossibilities as gospel. All progress of the human race has come from people pushing, risking, challenging, and disrupting.

Having kids who do that might seem like a nightmare. But that's the paradox. If you beat into your kids the ideas of obedience, of respect, of deference, of being seen and not heard, you might have a quiet home — but you will ensure a complacent, miserable future, depriving them and the world of so much progress and originality.

Teach them to challenge things. Show them what it looks like by challenging your own beliefs and the beliefs and practices of your peers.

Use AI to Find a Job and Prepare to Get Hired

This step-by-step guide walks through using AI to find real openings, compare them with your experience, tailor your resume, and practice for an interview — following one fictional applicant through the full process first.

  1. Choose one target job title, two related titles, and your limits for location, work setup, salary, and experience.
  2. Ask ChatGPT to find five current openings. Check each role on the employer's own careers page before adding it to your list.
  3. Compare one verified job description with a redacted copy of your resume. Ask AI to show strong matches, partial matches, and missing requirements.
  4. Tailor the resume and write a short cover letter using only facts you can support. Review every new claim before saving or applying.
  5. Practice eight interview questions, one at a time, then add the application and next follow-up date to your tracker.

Pro tip: Ask AI to show the evidence behind every resume change. A keyword is useful only when it describes work you've actually done.
