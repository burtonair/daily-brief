Moderna and Merck's Personalized Cancer Vaccine Clears Late-Stage Trials

The mRNA vaccine, paired with Merck's immunotherapy drug Keytruda, gave melanoma patients a longer recurrence-free window and cut the risk of the cancer spreading — sending Moderna's stock up 177% and Merck's up 13%. Morning Brew

Moderna and Merck announced a breakthrough in late-stage trials of their jointly developed, personalized cancer vaccine yesterday, giving hope to melanoma patients living in fear of a recurrence and sending each company's stock soaring. The announcement pushed Moderna's stock up 177% to $174, while Merck's shares jumped 13%.

In the trials, the mRNA injection coupled with Keytruda gave patients a longer window before their melanoma returned after surgery and reduced the risk of the cancer spreading to other parts of the body. But the treatment's bespoke nature — as opposed to a one-size-fits-all approach — makes it expensive to produce: after removing a tumor, scientists use a biopsy and blood sample to build a genetic profile of the cancer, then encode its unique mutations into an mRNA vaccine that works alongside Keytruda to keep the cancer in remission.

Moderna and Merck are now studying the treatment's effect on other tumors, including lung, bladder, and renal cell carcinoma. The results have yet to be independently reviewed — the companies plan to present the data at an upcoming international medical meeting — but Moderna CEO Stéphane Bancel hopes to have the treatment available by 2027.

You Are Rich (If You Can Do This. If You Do This.)

Not everyone can do it. Some people are paddling furiously just to stay afloat — working multiple jobs, fighting their way out of poverty or addiction or generational trauma. These parents are struggling mightily just to provide for their kids. As a result, they can't make it to every practice. They can't sit in that long pickup line after school. They can't spend chunks of the summer — or the holidays — just doing family stuff.

But you? You could…if you wanted. If you made this more of a priority. You have the luxury and the power of making your own schedule. You can afford to delegate. You could pass on opportunities. No one would die if you were less reachable—although your ego might take a hit.

But you will have to be willing to pay that price, to exercise that power. You will have to decide that the kids are the center of your life — not conferences, not meetings, not being "in the room where it happens."

And if you can do that, if you can make the decision to put your kids first, then you'll understand what it is to be truly rich.

Use the Loop Method for Better ChatGPT Results

Improve any repeated ChatGPT workflow with the review-based "Loop Method" — built to sharpen video editing and image generation skills, but it works on anything.

Uber's Tiger Team Playbook for AI ROI

After blowing through its entire 2026 AI budget in four months and declaring the "tokenmaxxing era" over, Uber's CTO detailed what the company did next: small "Agentic Pods" pairing an AI-proficient engineer with a domain expert on 10-day sprints. The Rundown AI

Each Agentic Pod is just two people — one engineer, one domain expert from Finance, Marketing, or Ops — who shadow the expert, build alongside them, and ship. The result: a financial pacing report that used to take a key player two full days now runs in 10 minutes.

Rundown educator Nate Grahek's takeaway: your first AI projects have to earn you the right to swing bigger. Enterprise needs to start acting more like bootstrappers — quick, pragmatic efficiency wins, not flashy long-roadmap bets.

Replit Adds Free Mode for Routine AI Work

Replit launched Free Mode, letting its $20 and $100 paid plans run everyday chats and tasks on OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Luna without eating up user credits — crediting Luna's 80% price cut for making the math work. The Rundown AI

The $20/month Core plan scales as much as 30x usage with Free Mode, adding 30 hours a month of chat on the efficient model. Bigger builds still burn credits in higher-performance modes, with the agent able to route to OAI's Sol for a harder step before handing back to Luna.
