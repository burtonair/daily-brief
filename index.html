Nothing Scarier Than a Big Tech Sell-Off: Nasdaq Posts Worst Day Since June

The Nasdaq Composite fell 2.86% Thursday, its worst single-day performance since June, as investors soured on massive AI spending after several Big Tech earnings reports came in soft. Morning Brew

With tech stocks selling off, the Nasdaq 100 shed as much as 4.5% yesterday, falling into its third correction this year. The conclusion investors and analysts kept landing on: a lot of money is going into AI, and not enough is coming out.

Google parent Alphabet had its worst day ever Thursday — about $475 billion in market value vanished after it reported slower-than-expected search revenue growth and a swing to a roughly $9 billion free-cash-flow loss. Amazon fell 1.41% after disclosing it spent even more than expected on AI last quarter while online shopping growth slowed. No one had it worse than Intel, though: its stock cratered 28.58%, its worst day in 50 years, on weak PC demand amid heavy AI spending. The US government's 10% stake in Intel lost about $12 billion in a single session.

Big picture: AI spending is reshaping the economy, and tech companies see it as the key to dominating the future — but investors are growing nervous about whether it will actually pay off.
