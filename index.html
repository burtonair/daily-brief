Sony and Rockstar Ditch Physical Discs as Digital-Only Era Arrives

Sony will sell new PlayStation games as digital downloads only starting in 2028, and Grand Theft Auto VI — the decade's biggest release — will skip physical media entirely, accelerating a shift that has gamers and regulators questioning what "owning" a game even means anymore. Morning Brew

Sony will sell new PlayStation games as digital downloads only starting in 2028, the company announced earlier this year — partly because that's how most people buy games now anyway, and partly because premium titles are increasingly too large to cram onto a single disc. Grand Theft Auto VI will also be digital-only. Morning Brew

Consumer advocates argue the shift obscures an uncomfortable truth: you don't actually own a digital game, you're buying a revocable license to play it. Sony is now facing a proposed class-action lawsuit claiming the company doesn't make that distinction clear enough. Morning Brew

Meanwhile, a cloud gaming surge is coming, Take-Two's CEO predicted — helpful as the AI boom jacks up console prices via ballooning hardware and memory demands. But it also turns gaming into a subscription service beholden to price hikes, like Microsoft's recent move to impose monthly streaming limits on Xbox Game Pass. Morning Brew

Why it matters: gaming companies may turn to in-game advertising to offset rising cloud costs and give players a way around usage caps — Xbox already started testing ad-supported game streaming in July. Morning Brew

How Nintendo Went From Playing Cards to the Switch

Before it was a gaming giant, Nintendo spent six decades as Japan's top maker of hand-painted Hanafuda playing cards. It didn't move into gaming devices until after its 1962 IPO, and didn't break through until 1981's Donkey Kong introduced the world to Mario. Today it trails Sony's PlayStation in the console race but still leads in physical game sales. Morning Brew

You Can Get a College Scholarship for Playing Video Games

High schoolers are landing esports scholarships the same way athletes do — and the numbers are getting serious.

The National Association of Collegiate Esports, founded in 2016, now counts 200+ schools and 16,000 student-athletes, with scholarships typically ranging from $20,000 to $30,000. Morning Brew

At one recent New Jersey recruiting event, 29 colleges extended 181 offers totaling $99,000 in scholarships, including two full rides. Morning Brew

Gaming and Hollywood Keep Blurring the Line

Studios are chasing gaming IP and revenue from both directions.

Paramount unveiled its own gaming studio in June to turn its film and TV library into video games; Warner Bros. Discovery, which Paramount is trying to acquire, already has its own in-house studio too. Morning Brew
