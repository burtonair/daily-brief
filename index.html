Sports • NFL

The Effort to Take American Football Global

NFL commissioner Roger Goodell says he has “no doubt” the league will someday field an internationally based team, as the league schedules a record nine games abroad this season. Morning Brew

A record nine international NFL games are scheduled for the upcoming season, up from just two in 2021, spanning four continents with Paris and Rio de Janeiro debuting as host cities. The league hopes filling stadiums that typically host soccer will convert more foreigners into regular NFL watchers, leading to lucrative media rights deals. Germany is often floated as the top candidate for a first non-US team — German Super Bowl viewership grew to 1.87 million this year from 1.55 million in 2025 — while London has hosted the most international games, and some executives argue Canada or Mexico would make more logistical sense.

Skeptics note the logistical headaches: regular overseas travel could tax players, and more than half of NFL players say they wouldn't join a foreign team, per a 2023 Athletic survey. History offers a cautionary tale — a European NFL offshoot that ran from 1991 to 2007 failed to build a fandom amid team relocations and thin talent, costing the league $400 million in losses.

Sports • Fantasy Football

How Fantasy Football Became a Reality

In the early 1960s, Raiders part-owner Bill “Wink” Winkenbach invented a way to own a fake team — sparking a fantasy revolution. Morning Brew

In 1962, Winkenbach, Raiders publicist Bill Tunnell, and reporter Scotty Stirling drew up rules for the first fantasy football league, drafting positions like two kickers and two fullbacks and gathering results by hand from newspaper box scores. The following year, the Greater Oakland Professional Pigskin Prognosticators League held its first official draft, with Houston Oilers QB/kicker George Blanda going first overall. The internet has since turned it into a multi-billion-dollar industry with tens of millions of participants.
