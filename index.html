Rewrite Your LinkedIn Profile Without Letting AI Embellish It

ChatGPT can rewrite your LinkedIn profile for the job you're moving into, but left alone, it tends to round your story up. Here's how to get an honest rewrite instead:

  1. Copy your LinkedIn headline, About section, one role, and your skills into a note as text, along with two or three job posts you'd apply for.
  2. In ChatGPT, open a Temporary chat set to Unpersonalized, and paste a prompt asking for up to 5 questions first, all at once, and stating "Don't invent numbers, tools, titles, or results," with your profile and the job posts underneath.
  3. Answer every question, including what you haven't done yet. Then ask for three headlines under 220 characters and an About section under 2,600 characters.
  4. Ask for five experience bullets that start with a verb, plus up to 10 skills your experience actually shows.
  5. Have ChatGPT check everything it wrote in a table, flag anything you didn't say, and use only the corrected versions before you leave the chat.

Pro tip: Turn off "Share profile updates" in LinkedIn's Settings & Privacy before you paste anything in, so your network isn't notified of every edit.
