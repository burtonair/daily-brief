OpenAI’s ‘Astra’ Cracks 10 Long-Standing Math Problems

An unreleased frontier model solved a clutch of problems that have stumped mathematicians for decades — including one dating to 1999 — for about $2,000 in tokens, reigniting debate over whether AI-authored proofs deserve human accolades.

OpenAI revealed that Astra, an internal version of its next major model family, solved 10 long-open math and computer science problems (one nearly 30 years old), spanning geometry, group theory, and quantum complexity. Rundown AI

Astra proved non-sofic groups exist — building the first symmetry structure that can’t be imitated by any finite shuffle, an exception hunted since 1999 — and also solved Alain Connes’s rigidity conjecture, Ehrhart’s volume conjecture, and three problems from Paul Erdős’s list that hadn’t moved in a decade. Each proof was verified in Lean, with the full chain-of-thought released, at a cost of roughly $2,000 in tokens for all successful runs. Rundown AI

Within 24 hours, Anthropic’s Levent Alpoge claimed he reproduced five of the 10 proofs using Fable, running on a generic prompt with no internet access. The community is now debating whether machine-authored proofs deserve Fields Medal-level credit — a question that only grows bigger as AI capable of cracking decades-old problems cheaply moves into domains like drug discovery and materials science. This Is What They Love About Them

Kids love their grandparents — which is a little baffling, since these are the same people who raised their own parents. Part of it is presents, part of it is fewer rules. But the real thing good grandparents do is treat kids differently: they take them seriously.

“He always took me seriously,” one of Ulysses S. Grant’s granddaughters said of the general. “I felt promoted and inclined to live up to my new position as his companion.” Kids get a special chair at grandma’s house, rituals reserved just for visits, someone who’s genuinely excited to hear the latest news and gets down on the floor to play.

None of that has to be unique to grandparents. You don’t have to spoil your kids, but you can treat them like something other than a little accessory or a subordinate — you can treat them like the person they are. They won’t love you for it quite as much as they love their grandparents, but they’ll appreciate it all the same.

Run Your Workday by Voice With ChatGPT

Turn a spoken request for an update into a finished PDF report and send it to your team — without touching your keyboard.

  1. Open the ChatGPT app, create a project, and start Voice. Connect your workflow parts — work data, communication apps, storage.
  2. Ask ChatGPT to “scaffold” a folder for drafting reports in Markdown and finalizing them as PDFs, so the process repeats.
  3. Tell ChatGPT what report the team needs and which inputs/KPIs it requires — e.g., “Build a Markdown draft of [report] using only [approved source] and include [required fields].”
  4. Review, trim anything unnecessary, then have it generate the final PDF, save it to the deliverables folder, and draft the handoff message.
