Bob Iger and Josh Kushner Buy the Lakers for a Record $12.5 Billion

A group led by Disney's former CEO and Jared Kushner's younger brother is buying the controlling stake in the Los Angeles Lakers, topping the record set just a year ago by the team's last sale — and capping a fast flip by outgoing owner Mark Walter, who is under federal investigation for alleged tax fraud. Morning Brew

A group led by Bob Iger and Josh Kushner will buy the controlling majority stake in the Lakers for a record-breaking $12.5 billion, ESPN reported yesterday. If the NBA approves it, the deal would be the highest valuation in history for any sports team, topping the mark set roughly a year ago when businessman Mark Walter bought a majority stake from the Buss family for ~$10 billion.

Walter's decision to flip the team so quickly comes as his business empire faces potential legal peril: the Guggenheim Partners chair and Dodgers owner is under federal investigation for alleged tax fraud involving billions in loans to his companies, and selling the Lakers should help him raise cash to pay those loans down, per Bloomberg.

Why it matters: Iger, who ran Disney (the NBA's broadcast partner) for most of the past two decades, advises Thrive Capital, the AI-focused VC firm run by Kushner's younger brother. The duo was reportedly eyeing an NBA Las Vegas expansion before pivoting to the Lakers deal — and the recently LeBron James-less team now looks to reshape around Luka Dončić.

Turn Customer Feedback Into 100 Ad Hooks With ChatGPT

Turn a pile of customer comments into a spreadsheet full of ad ideas — extracting pain points, generating copy and image prompts, and packaging the winners into a reusable app:

  1. Make a folder on your PC, create a ChatGPT Work project inside it to keep the CSVs, images, and code, then grab 10-20 customer comments or reviews.
  2. Have ChatGPT turn them into a CSV with the person, pain point, frustrating moment, desired outcome, objection, and where the comment came from.
  3. Make another CSV turning every pain point into an ad headline, CTA, and square feed-image idea, then see and pick which angles are interesting.
  4. Generate initial ads and optimize them with feedback. Once finalized, ask ChatGPT to scaffold an app that runs the CSV with an OpenRouter key and saves ad images in folders.

Columnist | Daily Dad

You Could Do More and You Know It

You don't see why it matters. You don't see why it's such a big deal. It's just dishes. It's just a gesture. It's just some party planning. Who cares? Not you — because you assume someone else will care, that your spouse will take care of it. In a sense, that is why it matters: because you think it doesn't, and are therefore deciding to put it on someone else's plate, someone else's shoulders to carry.

Things would be better at home, for your kids, for you, if you did care. If you noticed more. If you handled those dishes, if you put that away, if you took over that party planning, if you cleaned up. What would it look like if your kids had two parents who were on top of it? How much better would your relationship be if things were more evenly distributed — if your partner felt like they could count on you, if they occasionally felt taken care of?

You could do more. You should do more.
