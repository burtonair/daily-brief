Use Gemini Canvas to Turn a Google Sheet Into a Shareable Dashboard

Google’s new Gemini Canvas tool might be the fastest way yet to prototype inside Google Workspace — no coding required, and it updates live for anyone you share the link with. Rundown AI

  1. Open a spreadsheet (like a project tracker or content calendar) in Google Sheets. Launch Gemini in a new tab, click the + button, and select Canvas.
  2. Prompt Gemini: “Make me an interactive dashboard using the data from tabs one and two.”
  3. When it finishes, copy the Canvas link and paste it into your browser to view the dashboard outside the chat.
  4. To edit, just talk to Gemini in the chat like normal — anyone you’ve shared the link with sees the updated version too.

Pro tip: Gemini can also build fast slide decks in Canvas — ask for slides from the same tabs, then export to Google Slides or a PDF. Full guide. Rundown AI

Google’s AI Chips Are Headed to Orbit

Trillium TPUs launch next week aboard a SpaceX rideshare mission as part of Project Suncatcher, testing whether space could one day host solar-powered data centers.

The chips will fly on SpaceX’s Transporter-18 mission with satellite firm Planet, testing survival against radiation, vibration, and heat; a satellite in the right orbit can capture 8x more solar energy than on Earth, though cooling is harder since heat can only escape via radiators in a vacuum. Google announced the plan, envisioning laser-linked satellite clusters starting with a two-satellite test in 2027. Rundown AI

Why it matters: Data center buildouts have faced fierce pushback over noise, grid strain, and rising energy bills — if orbital compute works, it could relieve that pressure, though OpenAI’s Sam Altman remains skeptical it can scale this decade. Rundown AI
