OpenAI Resets the Cost Curve — GPT‑5.6 Gets Cheaper Because Its Own AI Rewrote the Code Behind It

What happens when an AI model gets good enough to lower its own price? OpenAI just gave the industry its first real answer, slashing rates on its GPT‑5.6 family after its Sol model rewrote the GPU code powering the efficiency gains.

OpenAI announced new price cuts to its GPT-5.6 model family, including an 80% cost reduction for its already cost-effective Luna variant, moving it to the top of the intelligence charts on cost per task. Luna now runs at $0.20/$1.20 per million tokens, while the mid-tier Terra model comes in at $2/$12. Rundown AI

OpenAI published research showing its Sol model rewrote its own GPU code to make the 5.6 models 15% more efficient while cutting serving costs by 20% — gains the company says it passed straight to customers. Sol’s own rates stayed flat, but a new Fast mode brings 2.5x speeds in the API at double the price. Sam Altman said OpenAI wants to “offer the best price/intelligence tradeoff at every level,” with Chinese and open models keeping the pressure on. Rundown AI

The timing is pointed: Google’s new Gemini Flash release last week was aimed at cost and efficiency, but OpenAI’s cuts land at a much higher intelligence tier. Intelligence too cheap to meter isn’t here yet, but rock-bottom prices at this capability level open up a lot of new workflow options. Rundown AI

Turn Any Idea Into an AI-Powered Site With Lovable

A step-by-step for going from a rough app idea to a working AI-powered site without writing a giant technical prompt — full guide here.

  1. Open ChatGPT, attach a reference screenshot, and describe the app in plain English. Let it ask a few useful questions, but keep the first version focused on a single journey.
  2. Ask ChatGPT to turn the conversation into concise feature requirements and a brand brief for Lovable — colors, feel, and typography — then strip out any extra features before copying the handoff.
  3. Create a Lovable project, attach the same screenshot, and paste the handoff specs.
  4. In Connectors → Cloud AI, enable Lovable’s built-in AI to test and refine the app. Once the core loop works, add accounts, data storage, and sharing — built around a validated product, not an assumption.

Jersey Patches Are Coming for Your College Football Team

Ohio State and Notre Dame just signed the biggest corporate-logo jersey deals in college sports history.

Ohio State and Notre Dame agreed to massive multi-year deals to place corporate logos on their jerseys starting as soon as this weekend. Ohio State will reportedly collect $17 million a year from JPMorgan Chase, while Notre Dame’s SoFi patch deal is worth $18–20 million annually. The new NIL revenue stream follows the NCAA granting schools permission to sell up to two logos on regular-season apparel plus one more for conference championships. Arkansas (Tyson Foods), Wisconsin (Culver’s), and LSU (Woodside Energy) have similar deals lined up, and Penn State could be next, with Pennsylvania-based Utz and Hershey among the front-runners.
