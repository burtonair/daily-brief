It Shouldn't Come At Their Expense

You want them to go to a good school. You want them to get every opportunity. You want them to succeed. You want them to be safe.

You want these things for your kids, and you will do whatever it takes to get those opportunities for them. That's what being a parent is. It's biological. It's natural. It's beautiful.

But of course, if taken too far, it can also become ugly and evil. Are you fighting for these things at the expense of other families? Are you so focused on your kids that you're forgetting, as James Baldwin said, that they're all our children?

When we focus on our family, it's easy to lose sight of the fact that we belong to this thing called society. It's easy to lose track of what we owe not just our kids but each other; what we owe their generation and the generation after that. We should want the best for our kids. Of course we should. But not at the expense of someone else's. Not at the expense of the world they're going to inherit.
