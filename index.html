They Are Always Listening

You're just trying to get them to behave. You're just trying to get them to listen to you, to stop hitting their sister, to take school seriously, to whatever. So you speak firmly, even harshly. You're tired, you've had this conversation a thousand times, you're not as kind as you could be. You're just joking maybe, just giving your honest opinion about this or that.

But you know what you're doing, really? You're putting a voice in their head. That's how this happens.

Everything we say, every interaction we have with our kids is shaping them. How we speak to them informs how they will speak to themselves. If you want proof of this, think about all the complexes and scripts you picked up from your parents — maybe things you're working on in therapy right now, decades later.

So while you can, before it's too late…catch yourself. Make this interaction a kind one, a patient one, a friendly one. Speak to them the way you'd want them to speak to themselves. Because it's not a matter of if they will internalize the things they heard growing up, it's a matter of what they will internalize. Put a good voice in their head.

Build a Shared Tracker in Claude Sheets

Claude now builds its own spreadsheets, turning a description into a table and leaving the formulas in the cells so you can check every number. Here's how to build a campaign tracker with status and budget, then share it for editing.

  1. Go to claude.ai/artifacts, click the green Sheets tile under Make something new, and describe the tracker: every column, dropdown values, and cells with formulas.
  2. Click a total and read the formula bar. Then select the table, click Ask Claude, and ask it to point out any cell holding a typed number instead of a formula, since only formulas update when Spent changes.
  3. Ask Claude to highlight tasks that are past their due date and not Done. Then paste your own tasks over the samples and have Claude delete the leftovers.
  4. Click Share, invite a teammate with Can edit, and leave General access on Only you. On paid plans, people can edit cells but can't ask Claude for changes.

Pro tip: Before any big change, open Share, then the Export tab, and download an Excel copy. If an edit goes wrong, you still have the tracker as it was. For a bad build, Undo on the "Claude updated" notice takes the whole thing back.

Rowan's Corner: How I Use AI to Coach My Calls

The Rundown's Rowan Cheung has AI tear apart his own performance after every high-stakes call — a habit he says has become one of his most useful. The Rundown AI

The setup: record meetings with Granola, connect the transcripts to Claude, and instead of pulling summaries and action items, have it look backward at his own performance. Among the analyses he runs — his talk ratio, flagged whenever it passes 50%; every moment he dodged a hard topic; a commitment log of everything promised; and an objection library with a stronger answer drafted for each pushback.
