Get Started With ChatGPT dot, OpenAI's New Agent

This guide walks through setting up dot, OpenAI's new always-on agent, and putting it to work on a morning project brief. Note: dot is currently rolling out to paid Pro and Business Premium users.

  1. Open ChatGPT's desktop app or web version and follow the dot setup (you can't create a dot on mobile). Leave local-computer access off for your first test.
  2. In the mobile app, open Customize → Custom rules and select "Ask" before taking action. Keep sending, publishing, and purchases outside the task.
  3. Use + to attach project notes and prompt: "Use only [FILE] to prepare a morning project brief. Cite the source for each fact. Reply here only."
  4. Check facts: owners, dates, and anything it can't confirm. Once a one-off brief passes, ask for a weekday brief at 8:30 AM and manage it under "Scheduled."

Pro tip: Before scheduling, attach an update that changes a deadline and ask for a revised brief — a smooth first answer tells you little about how your dot handles changes. Read the full guide
