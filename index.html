Someday You'll Want Them to Ask You for Stuff

It’s never enough. It’s rarely presented with a “please” or followed by a “thank you.” It’s exhausting how much our kids ask us for stuff — can I have this, can you buy me that, can we go out to eat. So yeah, it’s frustrating. You worry they’re getting spoiled or entitled, you wonder about their manners, and you weigh which request to grant and which to refuse while trying to keep your cool.

It’s worth remembering that this, like all things, is a phase. What they want is a phase — right now it’s Pokémon cards or Robux; later it’ll be gas money or a car. But wanting all of this from you is a phase, too.

They are dependent on you for only so long. They look to you for only so long. Soon enough, you’ll miss all these requests. Soon enough, you’ll be sitting by the phone wishing they’d call and ask if you’d take them to dinner — looking for ways to help them, being extra generous at Christmas, sending little things you know they need for their apartment.

We’ll always want to care for our kids and want them to look to us for help. So even if it’s frustrating now, even if it drives you crazy every time you’re at the store with them, take a second to remember this: someday soon, you’ll give anything for them to ask you for something.
