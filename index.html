You Are Going to Regret Being on Autopilot

You just do what you've always done. You just do what other parents are doing. You do what your parents have done. You're busy, so you don't think much about it. You're just trying to survive, so you shrug it off.

Like most parents — especially in the rhythms and exhaustion of life — you're on autopilot. And that's understandable. You're juggling what feels like a million tasks. Autopilot might be fine if you're an airline pilot, or if your car has hands-free cruise control. But when it comes to your kids, being on autopilot means you are not present. You are not making conscious choices. You are handing over this moment, this experience, to something or someone else.

And you're probably going to regret that. It doesn't age well. They are growing up. They are watching and learning from the day-to-day example you set.

You only get one chance to be a parent to your kids at their current age. They will never be this young again. Don't miss it because you're on autopilot — because one day, you're going to regret outsourcing the moments, the lessons, and the relationship only you could have built.

Make Your AI Usage Go Further

Claude Pro's $20 plan runs on a five-hour and a weekly limit, and long chats and high-effort use both burn through it faster. Here's how to set up a leaner chat:

  1. Click your account button at the bottom of Claude's sidebar, then Usage, to note when the current session and weekly bars reset.
  2. In a new chat, click the model button under the chat box and set Effort to Low. Under + and Connectors, switch off apps the chat won't use.
  3. Prompt: "Don't start yet. I want to [task]. Ask me up to five smart multiple-choice questions about what I want. After that, sketch the plan and wait for my go-ahead."
  4. Send your go-ahead and every change in one message. If a reply is missed, edit your message instead of sending a correction.
  5. When a chat runs long, ask for a handoff summary under 150 words and paste it into a new chat.

Pro tip: Using another AI assistant? Skip the Claude-specific settings and keep the habits — the sketch-first prompt, the one-message templates, and the handoff work in any chatbot.
