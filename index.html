Fed's Warsh Hints Interest Rates Might Go Up

Fed Chair Kevin Warsh said the central bank will have "work to do" if inflation doesn't cool, in his most revealing comments yet on where rates might head next. Morning Brew

Speaking at the Fed's yearly Jackson Hole symposium, Warsh said inflation remains above the Fed's 2% target and that he doesn't see recent data showing meaningful improvement. He argued the Fed should focus on inflation rather than the job market, which he called stable, and that rate hikes are the main tool for achieving price stability. Wall Street took it as a signal the Fed is leaning toward raising borrowing costs.

The trading odds of a rate hike at next month's Fed meeting jumped from 35% to 58% shortly after Warsh finished speaking, per CME Group data. The 2-year Treasury yield rose from 4.24% to as much as 4.36% in the hours afterward, while the 30-year yield dipped slightly after soaring to a 19-year high last week. The Fed has one more inflation reading to weigh before its September meeting.

Coyote vs. Acme Finally Hits Theaters After Warner Bros. Tried to Kill It

Three years after being shelved as a tax writeoff, the completed film opened this weekend to rave reviews. Morning Brew

Warner Bros. Discovery greenlit the film in 2020, but new leadership killed it in 2023 as part of a $115 million tax writeoff even though it had already wrapped and screened well with critics. The backlash prompted Warner Bros. to sell the worldwide distribution rights to Ketchup Entertainment for $50 million; Ketchup then built its marketing campaign around the movie's near-death story. The film has reeled in a solid $1.7 million in previews and is tracking for a $15 million opening weekend.
