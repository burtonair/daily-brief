Tech Stocks Get Pummeled as the Post-Midterm AI Jitters Deepen

The Nasdaq cratered more than 5% as investors priced in a Democratic Congress that campaigned on slowing the AI data center buildout — the worst S&P 500 session since April's tariff shock. Morning Brew

Stocks were in freefall Thursday after a Wednesday upswing, as investors weighed what election wins by AI-skeptical Democrats would mean for the data center buildout. The tech-heavy Nasdaq dropped more than 5%, trading over 10% below its recent high, while the S&P 500 closed more than 4% in the red — its worst day since Trump's tariff announcements in April 2025. The prospect of a Democratic Congress tightening AI regulation, alongside newly elected Democratic governors pausing data center construction in hubs like Texas and Georgia, piled onto investors' existing AI skepticism.

Shares of data center suppliers took the hardest hit: the Philadelphia semiconductor index plunged more than 12%, Nvidia and memory chip supplier Sandisk closed down 9% and 22% respectively, and the selloff spread to software names that rode the AI wave, like Palantir and CrowdStrike, both down nearly 10%. The VIX — Wall Street's "fear gauge" — soared 69%, with Goldman Sachs traders telling Bloomberg they were flooded with calls from investors asking how deep the rout might go. It was a good day for safe havens: gold, Treasury bonds, and the Swiss franc all rose.

Is this a bubble bursting? Some of the selling traces to the political shift and underwhelming earnings guidance from chipmaker Qualcomm, but some analysts argue it may also be an overcorrection — Ed Yardeni of Yardeni Research told CNBC that slowing the data center buildout now could help prevent a bigger AI crash down the road.

Every Child Is Precious

Yes, you care about your kid more than anything. Yes, you put your family first. Of course you do — it's the most basic biological thing there is. But it should be said that the most basic moral thing there is, is understanding that everyone feels the same way. That everyone's child is precious to them, and that therefore, every child is precious.

"The children are always ours, every single one of them, all over the globe," James Baldwin famously said, "and I am beginning to suspect that whoever is incapable of recognizing this may be incapable of morality."

As we've said here before, the key to a functioning society and a good world is to strike a balance between our kids and our kids. We have to take care of our family while also expanding our definition of the family to be as big and inclusive as possible — we want our neighborhood to flourish, our country to flourish, for everyone in the world to be safe and secure, to go to bed on a full stomach.

Because if you don't, if that doesn't register with you, well, there's something wrong with you.

Use AI to Find a Job and Prepare to Get Hired

The Rundown's full guide walks through using AI to find real openings, compare them with your experience, tailor your resume, and practice for an interview — with one fictional applicant carrying through the full process so you can follow along before using your own details:

  1. Choose one target job title, two related titles, and your limits for location, work setup, salary, and experience.
  2. Ask ChatGPT to find five current openings, checking each role on the employer's own careers page before adding it to your list.
  3. Compare one verified job description with a redacted copy of your resume, and ask AI to show strong matches, partial matches, and missing requirements.
  4. Tailor the resume and write a short cover letter using only facts you can support, reviewing every new claim before saving or applying.
  5. Practice eight interview questions one at a time, then add the application and next follow-up date to your tracker.

Pro tip: Ask AI to show the evidence behind every resume change — a keyword is only useful if it describes work you've actually done.
