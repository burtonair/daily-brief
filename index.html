Report: Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Was Targeted by an Alleged Chinese Spy

The New York Times reported that U.S. officials found Alice Huang, an exec at Singapore-based data center firm Megaspeed, was allegedly passing intelligence to Beijing while maintaining close ties with Jensen Huang — with President Trump briefed about a year ago. The Rundown AI

It's unclear what Ms. Huang passed to Beijing or its value, but officials worried she could offer insight into Trump's view on chip sales to China, and into Nvidia itself. Officials didn't believe Jensen knew of her alleged role when Trump was briefed; he never got the defensive briefing often given to U.S. citizens targeted by spies.

Megaspeed — already under a separate U.S. probe for steering Nvidia chips to China — bought at least $4.8B in Nvidia gear via a sister firm and remains an Nvidia cloud partner. Nvidia called Jensen's meetings with Ms. Huang routine, saying customers get ordinary business info and “zero information relevant to national security.” Megaspeed said outside counsel is investigating and it's cooperating with the U.S., while China called the claims “groundless smears and slanders.”

Why it matters: nothing has been publicly proven, including whether Ms. Huang passed anything to China. The immediate fallout may hit the loophole allowing firms like Megaspeed to rent Nvidia-powered computing from Southeast Asia to Chinese AI labs — one Trump reopened but may now close.

You're Doing This For Them? Really?

“Everything I do, I do for my kids.” Really? That's why you're at another conference in Las Vegas? That's why you're taking another overseas assignment? That's why you're never at home? That's why you're volunteering for extra work projects or why you never miss tee time on the weekends? A quick look at your finances, at the risks involved, at the time it takes you away gives the game away. This isn't about them. It's about you.

And look, you're allowed to have ambitions. You're allowed to have hobbies. You're allowed to want things for yourself. Just don't hide those choices behind your kids. Don't lie to yourself or them about it.

Because doing something for them should actually benefit them. It should be about them. And it's worth remembering that most of the time what they need isn't another promotion, another paycheck, or another accomplishment.

Mostly what they need is you.

Make ElevenLabs Voiceovers Sound More Natural

In this guide, you'll add Audio Tags to a short Eleven v4 voiceover, compare it with the plain version, and create a short two-speaker scene. Full guide.

  1. Open ElevenLabs Text to Speech, choose one voice, and select Eleven v4. Generate the guide's plain script and save it for comparison.
  2. Paste the tagged version without changing the spoken words. Keep the same voice and settings so you can hear the difference.
  3. Compare the opening, pace, and final line. If the delivery stays flat, make one cue more specific — change one thing at a time.
  4. If a tag gets read aloud, check the model and square brackets. Keep the direction short and place it before the words it should affect.
  5. Open Dialogue mode, add two voices, and generate the guide's exchange. Check the words, speakers, and timing before downloading the clips.

Pro tip: Save a plain version before adding tags. A more expressive read may still sound less natural.
