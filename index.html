Even This, You'll Miss

The yelling. The screaming. The complaining. The repeated, obnoxious requests. The crying. The tantrums. It's crazy to think, but it's true — someday, even this is something you'll miss. Someday the house will be quiet. Someday it will take them a few days to return your call. Someday you'll be doing all this alone.

Sure, some of that will be wonderful. It will be calmer. It will be all yours. It will also be devastatingly quiet. It will be boring. It will be lonely.

Right now, you have them. Right now, you're the one they come to with their complaints. Right now, you're the one they ask for stuff. Right now, you're at the center of it. That does not last forever. Enjoy it while you can. Be present for it while you can. Appreciate it while you can.

“Now is now. It can never be a long time ago,” wrote Laura Ingalls Wilder. Now is all we have. When you find yourself rushing, ask yourself what you are rushing toward — and remember, someday you would do anything to get back to this very moment you're wishing away.
