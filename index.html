KFC Dumps Pepsi for Coke After 40 Years

KFC said Thursday it’s making the switch from PepsiCo to Coca-Cola starting in January, ending a four-decade partnership as it chases younger customers' taste for customizable drinks like refreshers and dirty sodas. Morning Brew

KFC US President Catherine Tan-Gillespie told employees and franchisees the move will help the chain “innovate, meet changing tastes, and give customers more reasons to choose us.” KFC has slipped from No. 1 in US chicken sales in 2012 to fifth overall this year, per Technomic.

The loss stings for Pepsi, already in a tough place: its stock is down double digits over the past year, and GLP-1 drugs and healthier habits have dinged its salty-snack business. Coke now holds about 44% of the US soda market to Pepsi's ~26%, per Circana, after also poaching Culver's, Arby's, Panda Express, Marriott, and the Costco food court. Pepsi will keep supplying KFC's overseas markets.
