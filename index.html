AI Catches the Bug Threatening 70% of the Internet

OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol — already a generation behind the frontier — helped researchers uncover two critical, decade-old flaws in PHP, the code behind roughly 70% of the world's websites, triggering emergency patches, an NSA warning, and government site shutdowns across Europe. The Rundown AI Morning Brew

The find: Security firm Calif caught the first bug on Aug. 1 using GPT-5.6 Sol; Google-owned Wiz separately caught both weeks later with Sol and Gemini 4 Argon. OpenAI worked alongside PHP's maintainers for two months before Monday's announcement and yesterday's 10am ET patch release — over 10,000 lines of code to fix bugs that let hackers hijack servers with no login required.

Who's exposed: Basically any WordPress site. Finland and the Netherlands pulled government sites offline, and some Wikipedia pages, 4chan, and BBC pages went dark this week. Developers rated the threat severity 9.2 out of 10, and Cloudflare says it's "seeing early attacks emerging."

Why it matters: The bug was flagged years ago but dismissed as too complex for hackers to exploit manually — until AI untangled it in hours. Ami Luttwak of Wiz called it "the most severe AI vulnerability that was ever found," and OpenAI says it's still fixing several more significant vulnerabilities it hasn't disclosed yet. Why You Can't Afford to Procrastinate as a Parent

The project isn't due for a while. You can't seem to motivate yourself. So you put it off. Sure, you could be putting in the work on it now, but nobody's making you. The conversation is going to be tough. There's a huge mess to sort through. So you tell yourself you'll get to it later.

This is life. This is what we do—we procrastinate. And there are all the reasons that procrastination is lazy and inefficient and a failure of willpower. But you know what it also is? It's a failure of parenting.

As we've said before, good parenting is about slack. Kids need lots of it. They are full of surprises. They have endless needs. There is always something coming up…and it's usually last-minute.

This is why you can't procrastinate. This is why you need to be on top of your stuff. So you have the slack, the flexibility to be able to rush to grab them from school, or rush them to the hospital, or rush to that cool once-in-a-lifetime opportunity that came their way.

Nvidia's Factory-Built AI Data Centers Just Need Power

Nvidia's new DSX POD reference design lets partners assemble prefabricated AI data centers in modular containers around its Vera Rubin systems, cutting deployment from six months to as little as 14 days. 1% Better

  • Dell, HPE, Lenovo, Bull, Crusoe, DayOne, and power/cooling makers Eaton, Schneider Electric, and Vertiv are the delivery partners; Nvidia names no buyer yet.
