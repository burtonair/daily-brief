Let Them Learn

It would be easy to give them all the answers. It’s even fun to give them the answers. It would make things go faster, smoother, and let you get back to what you were doing. But you can’t. You just can’t.

The goal is to try to raise kids who know how to figure things out — which means you have to let them figure things out. John Stuart Mill recalled that in his own unique childhood education, supervised by his father, “anything which could be found out by thinking I was never told, until I had exhausted my efforts to find it out for myself.” His father didn’t withhold help entirely — he encouraged his son to take a crack at it first, to struggle with it, to not know until he either learned or learned how to learn. The boy had to try and fail before anyone came to the rescue.

Luctor et emergo — struggle and they emerge. We have to make our kids understand that they have what it takes to do this on their own, and if they don’t, the best way to get it is to develop it through experience, curiosity, and exploration. We hold some of our answers back not because we don’t love them but because we love them so much. We let them struggle because we believe in them, and because we believe even more in what will come out the other side.

Build a Live Sales Dashboard in Claude

In this guide, you’ll use Claude’s Dashboards feature to see which sales deals need attention — checking totals, filtering by owner, and testing whether new source data reaches the report.

  1. Open Claude with a paid account and connect an approved data source. Use a small test dataset and limit the connection to reading records.
